PLAN

Nav log tabs 
BRIEF (crosswind, glare, night check) · WEATHER (wind advisor, Fly-the-Future scrubber, TAF bars, clouds) · NOTAMS · LOG table.
TUNE / DIRECT on a VOR 
DIRECT = just fly the big amber heading. TUNE = classic CDI with TO/FROM, also showing the fly-heading.
FINAL panel (right) 
virtual PAPI lights, glidepath strip, TRACK/FLY/XW steering, voice callouts, landing perf card. Arm from an airport popup, Ground view, or FLY HUD.
CAMERA VIEW 
experimental rear-camera runway silhouette — non-conformal aid only.

Safety panels

Guardian (top-left in FLY) 
engine-out: dashed reachability envelope + ranked runways by margin & crosswind.
Ground view (bottom-left) 
appears when slow near an airport — runway diagram, approach gates, per-end head/crosswind, SET FINAL.
SOS 
fullscreen emergency card: squawk 7700, position copy/SMS, best three runways.
DR banner 
appears automatically if GPS degrades with a route active — shows last HDG/GS.
