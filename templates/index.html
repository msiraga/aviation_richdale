TOOL DIRECTORY

Every chip in the top toolbar also pops a short tip on first use. The "Show this every time" toggle in the tip controls whether the tip re-appears; otherwise the app remembers you've seen it. This sheet is the long-form reference.

Plan

PLAN 
the route composer (left sidebar). Three bordered groups: ROUTE (departure, arrival, en-route waypoints) · PERFORMANCE (altitude slider with quick-set chips, TAS, burn, unit, destination runway heading for crosswind) · EXECUTE (Compute / Simulate / Edit on map / scenario). The composer scrolls internally so the EXECUTE block is always reachable on short screens.

Map layers (header · MAP)

DARK/LIGHT/SAT/TOPO 
base map cycle. TOPO is the default — OpenTopoMap with roads, railways, relief and city labels (the closest thing to a VFR ground-reference chart we can ship without keys). DARK and LIGHT are CARTO; SAT is Esri World Imagery.
CHART 
official ENAIRE VFR chart overlay on top of your base map. First time per tile takes a few seconds, then cached.
VOR/DME 
navaid symbols + airport dots coloured by live METAR (green = VFR, blue = MVFR, red = IFR; see the legend top-left of the map).
RAIN 
precipitation radar (RainViewer), refreshes every 5 minutes.
RP 
Spanish VFR entry/exit reporting points from ENAIRE VAC charts. Also addressable from the composer as RP:NAME or ICAO:NAME.
TRF 
ADS-B traffic advisory via OpenSky — only transponding aircraft, coverage-limited, advisory only.
EARTH 
3D Earth with your great-circle itinerary, day/night terminator, X-RAY (see through the planet), LOCATE (fly back to route).
VFR CHART 
OpenFlightMaps overlay — only appears if OPENFLIGHTMAPS_TILE_URL is provisioned. Hidden otherwise (no nag).
AIRSPACE 
openAIP airspace polygons — only if OPENAIP_API_KEY is configured.

Weather

BRIEF 
right-side weather drawer with three heavy brief cards: live METAR/TAF per airport on your route (from NOAA AWC, no key) · SIGMET over Spain (from NOAA AWC, FIR-filtered) · SIGWX charts (AEMET frentes +0/+24/+36/+48/+60/+72, OpenData analysis and forecast maps if AEMET_API_KEY is set, Spain sigwx 6h via aerobrava). Tap any airport chip inside the drawer to open its dossier.

Safety panels

SOS 
full-screen emergency card: squawk 7700, position copy/SMS, best three runways reachable at your current altitude/glide.
Guardian (top-left in FLY) 
engine-out: dashed reachability envelope + ranked runways by margin and crosswind.
Ground view (bottom-left) 
appears when slow near an airport — runway diagram, approach gates, per-end head/crosswind, SET FINAL.
FINAL panel (right) 
virtual PAPI lights, glidepath strip, TRACK/FLY/XW steering, voice callouts, landing perf card. Arm from an airport popup, Ground view, or FLY HUD.
CAMERA VIEW 
experimental rear-camera runway silhouette — non-conformal aid only.
DR banner 
appears automatically if GPS degrades with a route active — shows last HDG/GS.

Safety systems (new)

Personal minimums 
the GO/CAUTION/NOGO stamp on the BRIEF tab. Tap it to edit your rules (ceiling, visibility, crosswind, wind, night). The verdict rebuilds from live METAR every time you compute a route. Persists in localStorage.
Stabilized approach siren 
in FINAL mode below 600 AGL, the system checks speed, bank, alignment and descent rate every 500 ms. On violation: voice "UNSTABILIZED. UNSTABILIZED. GO AROUND." + PAPI lights strobe red + toast. Edge-triggered so it doesn't spam.
Weight & Balance 
composer section with POH envelope canvas, sliders for pilot/passenger/baggage/fuel, and live CG computation. Green dot = in envelope, red = out. Defaults to C172.
Fuel danger banner 
HUD banner appears when remaining fuel drops below 30 minutes reserve. Amber "RESERVE 30 MIN · LOW FUEL" → red "FUEL EXHAUSTED — LAND NOW" when empty.

Modals & viewers

Lightbox 
click any chart image to open it full-screen. Wheel / +/- to zoom, drag to pan, 0 to reset, ESC or X to close. Works on every sigwx, analysis, forecast map and 6-hourly Spain sigwx image.
Airport dossier 
tap any airport chip (in the WX drawer, in the nav log BRIEF, or on the map) to open: live METAR + decoded summary + TAF (raw), NOTAMs within 5 NM, AD2 fields (runways, transition altitude/level, Tower/Ground/Approach/ATIS frequencies), and a link to the official ENAIRE chart page for the airport. ESC or X to close.
NAV log tabs 
BRIEF (crosswind, glare, night check) · WEATHER (wind advisor, Fly-the-Future scrubber, TAF bars, clouds — plus an "Open full weather brief" button) · NOTAMS · LOG table.
3D Earth 
drag rotate · pinch / wheel / +/- zoom · real day/night · amber tube = shortest path over the curved Earth.

Navigation

TUNE / DIRECT on a VOR 
DIRECT = just fly the big amber heading. TUNE = classic CDI with TO/FROM, also showing the fly-heading.
Edit trip on map 
inside the composer. Click the map to insert a waypoint within 30 NM of the route, click a marker to remove it, drag markers to reposition. The route recomputes 700 ms after you stop moving.

Simulation & training

Simulate flight (ghost flyer) 
deterministic point-mass replay with real per-leg wind samples, fuel burn and drift. Results: T+ / GS / FUEL / DRIFT chip in the top toolbar.
Scenario trainer 
plain / engine failure mid-route / engine failure late. After the failure the system asks for your decision (land straight ahead / turn back / pick an option) and scores it on landing.

Keyboard shortcuts

ESC 
close the top-most overlay (lightbox, dossier, help sheet, sim-toast, decision card).
+ / - 
zoom in/out inside the lightbox.
0 
reset lightbox zoom to 100%.
wheel / drag 
zoom / pan inside the lightbox.

Troubleshooting quickies

Charts return empty 
press DIAGNOSE on the SIGWX card; it prints per-endpoint HTTP status and byte counts so you can see exactly where it broke.
AEMET key was rejected 
open https://opendata.aemet.es → profile → "Obtención de la APIKEY" → use the site's copy button → paste into .env.txt replacing the whole value, then restart the server.
Python changes not visible 
Python changes need a server restart. Page-only changes (templates, static) hot-reload after a browser refresh.
Tiles missing or pink 
check that your machine can reach the public tile servers (basemaps.cartocdn.com, tile.opentopomap.org, server.arcgisonline.com, a.tile.opentopomap.org). Mixed content: served over HTTPS, tiles are HTTPS — no issue.
Full detail, data sources and limitations live in MANUAL.md at the project root. This app is decision support, not certified equipment. Always cross-check with official ENAIRE publications, AEMET briefings and ATC instructions.

Personal minimums

These are your rules, not legal limits. The verdict chip on the BRIEF tab colors LIVE: when any block fires, the verdict flips to CAUTION or NOGO with the reason. PIC always decides; this is a hard nudge, not an autopilot.

Defaults to certified VFR minimums categories. Edit responsibly and document your changes. The verdict uses live METAR via NOAA AWC at the moment you press CHECK.
